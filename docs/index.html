About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Robotics Engineering at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Qiuguo Zhu. I previously conducted research at Tsinghua University’s TEA Lab with Prof. Huazhe Xu. I will join Prof. Chang Liu’s research group at Peking University for my master’s studies in the fall.

My research focuses on robot learning for dexterous manipulation and whole-body control. I am interested in developing learning-based methods that enable robots to interact skillfully with objects and move reliably in the physical world.

Research Interests

  • Dexterous Manipulation: learning skills for precise and adaptable object interaction.
  • Whole-Body Control: learning and deploying coordinated motion policies for humanoid robots.
